Our Bakery Story

Sweet Haven Bakery was founded in 2015 with one goal: bake honest, flavorful food with local organic ingredients. We still hand-shape every loaf, proof every pastry overnight, and finish every cake to order.

Our Mission

Handcrafted, organic baked goods made fresh daily for neighbors and celebrations alike.

Why Choose Us

  • Organic flour, seasonal produce, and small-batch methods.
  • Friendly service and custom orders for events.
  • Award-winning sourdough and signature croissants.
