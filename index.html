Our Story

Sweet Haven Bakery started as a weekend market stand and grew into a beloved local shop at 123 Maple Street. Every recipe is made from thoughtfully sourced, organic ingredients and baked in-house each morning.

Our mission is simple: share handcrafted, wholesome baked goods that bring comfort and joy to every bite.
